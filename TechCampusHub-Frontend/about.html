About TechCampus_Hub

A college learning portal built for BCA, MCA and B.Tech students — notes, previous year papers, TNP resources and exam timetables in one place.

Introduction

What is TechCampus_Hub?

TechCampus_Hub is a college learning portal that gathers the material students actually need during the semester: subject-wise study notes, previous year question papers, TNP resources, the syllabus and exam timetables — organised by course, branch, year and semester.

Everything lives behind one simple account. Visitors can preview the latest uploads on the homepage; signed-in students open them in a secure in-browser PDF viewer, and a one-time UPI payment unlocks every note inside a course bundle.

The portal is intentionally lightweight: no apps to install, no clutter — open the site, pick your course, and start reading.

Our Mission

To make quality study material easy to find and easy to read for every college student — organised by course and semester, available on any device, and priced so that a single bundle opens an entire subject instead of charging for each file.

Our Vision

To become the go-to learning companion for BCA, MCA and B.Tech students — a portal where notes, papers, TNP resources and exam updates are always current, trustworthy and one tap away, so students spend their time learning instead of hunting for files.

Features

Everything the portal offers

Subject-wise Notes

Semester and subject-wise notes for every supported course, uploaded and reviewed by the admin.

Previous Year Papers

PYQ papers organised by course, semester and subject so exam revision follows a pattern instead of guesswork.

TNP Resources

TNP material kept in the same library as notes and papers — one search, one place for everything.

Syllabus & Timetables

The full syllabus and exam timetables, so you always know what is coming and when.

Secure PDF Viewer

Notes open inside the site in a built-in viewer with page and zoom controls — no downloads, no extra apps.

One-time UPI Unlock

Buy a bundle once with any UPI app and every note inside it opens for your account — no subscriptions.

Developer Information
