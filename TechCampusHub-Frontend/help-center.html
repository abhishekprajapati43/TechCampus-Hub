Login Issues

I can't sign in to my account.

Check that you are using the exact email address you registered with and that the password is correct (it is case-sensitive). If you still can't get in, use Forgot password? on the login page to set a new one.

I forgot my password — how do I reset it?

Open the login page and click Forgot password?, then follow the reset steps for your registered email. If the email never arrives, check your spam folder and contact us.

Why was I signed out on my new phone or laptop?

Your session is stored on the device and browser you sign in from, so a new device simply asks you to log in again with the same email and password. Your courses and unlocks stay attached to your account.

The page keeps sending me back to the login screen.

Your session has probably expired — sign in once more. If it happens immediately, clear the site's cookies and cache in your browser and try again. Still stuck? Reach out under Contact Information.

Payment Help

How do I pay for a course?

Open the course and start the purchase. Scan the QR code or pay the displayed UPI ID with any UPI app, then submit your transaction reference (UTR / Ref no.) at checkout. The payment is a one-time purchase for that bundle.

I paid, but my notes are still locked.

Every transaction reference is reviewed and approved before the bundle unlocks — this normally happens within a day. You can follow the status in Payment History. If it is still locked after approval time, contact us with your transaction reference and we will sort it out.

Where do I find my transaction reference (UTR)?

Open your UPI app, go to the payment you made, open Transaction details and copy the UTR / Ref no. — that is the number the checkout asks for.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once per bundle and every note inside that bundle opens for your account — there is no recurring charge.

Course Access Help

Which courses does the portal cover?

BCA, MCA and B.Tech — with subject-wise notes, previous year papers, TNP resources, syllabus and exam timetables. See About Us for the full picture.

Can I browse without an account?

Yes — visitors can browse the homepage previews, which show the title, subject and course of the latest uploads. Opening the material itself requires an account: Please login to access Notes, PYQs, TNP, and Syllabus.

Where do I find the courses I bought?

Sign in and open the Dashboard or My Courses from the header — everything unlocked for your account is listed there.

I paid on another device — how do I get access here?

Sign in on this device with the same email address. Purchases and unlocks follow your account, not the device.

Notes & PDF Viewer Help

How do I open a note or previous year paper?

Sign in, then open the resource from the homepage or your course. It loads in the built-in secure viewer right inside the browser — no extra app or extension is needed.

The viewer shows an error or never finishes loading.

Check your internet connection, refresh the page and open the resource once more. If it still fails, tell us the course, semester and note title under Contact Information and we will look into it.

How do I move between pages and zoom?

The viewer toolbar shows Page X of Y together with zoom out, zoom level and zoom in buttons. Scroll inside the viewer to read through the document.

Why can't I download, save or print the file?

The material opens inside the secure viewer so it stays with the students it was uploaded for. Use the viewer's page and zoom controls to read it on any screen.
